# BudeFood Merchant Registration Guide

A simple step-by-step guide for food businesses joining BudeFood.

## Before you start
Have these ready:
- Business name
- Business address
- Contact phone number
- Contact email address
- Bank account details for payouts
- Your current food menu
- Opening hours
- Business logo if you have one
- Food photos only if you want to use your own
- Stripe account details, or be ready to create a Stripe account during setup

## Step 1 — Open the BudeFood Merchant app
Open the BudeFood Merchant app on your Android phone, iPhone, tablet or computer.

Tap:

**REGISTER MY BUSINESS**

## Step 2 — Enter your business details
Enter:
- Business name
- Address
- Contact name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Type of business, for example:
  - Takeaway
  - Restaurant
  - Café
  - Bakery
  - Pub
  - Fish & chip shop
  - Indian
  - Chinese
  - Thai
  - Pizza

Tap:

**CONTINUE**

## Step 3 — Confirm your opening hours
Choose the days and times you normally open.

You can change these later at any time.

You will also have a quick merchant control to set:
- Open now
- Busy
- Closed
- Opening later

Tap:

**SAVE HOURS**

## Step 4 — Connect Stripe for payments
BudeFood uses Stripe Connect so payments can be automatically allocated to your business.

Tap:

**CONNECT STRIPE**

If you already have a Stripe account:
1. Sign in to Stripe.
2. Approve the connection to BudeFood.
3. Confirm the bank account you want payouts sent to.

If you do not have a Stripe account:
1. Tap **Create Stripe Account**.
2. Enter the business and identity information requested by Stripe.
3. Add the bank account where you want your BudeFood payouts sent.
4. Complete Stripe's verification steps.
5. Return to BudeFood.

Once connected, the merchant app should show:

**STRIPE CONNECTED**

BudeFood will automatically calculate the agreed platform commission and allocate the remaining merchant amount to your connected Stripe account.

## Step 5 — Add your menu using Smart Menu
This is designed to be the quickest part.

Tap:

**SMART AI**

Then tap:

**SCAN MENU**

Take a clear photo of your menu.

If your menu has more than one page, photograph each page.

BudeFood will read the photographs and prepare:
- Menu sections
- Item names
- Descriptions
- Prices
- Visible dietary information

Example:

**Fish**
- Cod & Chips — £12.95
- Haddock & Chips — £13.50

**Sides**
- Chips — £3.50
- Mushy Peas — £1.50

Nothing is published automatically.

## Step 6 — Check the menu
BudeFood shows the menu it has created.

Check:
- Item names
- Prices
- Descriptions
- Categories
- Spelling

Tap an item if anything needs changing.

When everything looks right, tap:

**APPROVE MENU**

Then:

**PUBLISH**

Your menu is now available in BudeFood.

## Step 7 — Add your own food photos if you want
Food photographs are optional.

BudeFood does not automatically add stock or AI-generated food photographs.

If you want a picture next to a menu item:
1. Open that menu item.
2. Tap **ADD PHOTO**.
3. Take a new photo or choose one of your own.
4. Check it.
5. Tap **USE THIS PHOTO**.

If you do not add a photo, the menu item simply appears as text.

## Step 8 — Add today's specials
If you have a specials board, tap:

**SMART AI → SCAN SPECIALS**

Take a photograph of the specials board.

BudeFood prepares the specials and prices for you.

Check them and tap:

**APPROVE & PUBLISH**

The specials appear near the top of your BudeFood storefront.

You can remove them again with one tap.

## Step 9 — Choose collection and delivery
Choose whether you offer:
- Collection
- BudeFood / WhyDrive delivery
- Both

If using WhyDrive delivery, BudeFood handles the delivery request through the connected driver network.

## Step 10 — Go live
Check your storefront preview.

Confirm:
- Business name
- Address
- Opening hours
- Menu
- Prices
- Collection / delivery options
- Stripe connected

Tap:

**GO LIVE**

Your business can now receive BudeFood orders.

# What happens when an order arrives?

The merchant app makes a clear alert.

Open the order and check the items.

Choose a preparation time, for example:
- Accept — 15 minutes
- Accept — 25 minutes
- Accept — 40 minutes

If the food is ready earlier than expected, tap:

**READY NOW**

or:

**CALL DRIVER NOW**

This tells the WhyDrive delivery system the order can be collected earlier.

If you need longer, update the preparation time so the driver is not sent too early.

---

# If something is sold out
Open the menu item and tap:

**SOLD OUT**

The item is immediately unavailable to customers.

When it is available again, tap:

**BACK IN STOCK**

---

# Reviews
Customers rate the food/outlet separately from the delivery.

The merchant rating is based on the food and outlet experience.

Delivery and driver feedback is handled separately so a delivery problem does not unfairly reduce the restaurant's rating.

For 1-star or 2-star merchant reviews, the customer must explain the reason.

Merchants can reply publicly to reviews from the Merchant app.

---

# The simple version

To join BudeFood:

1. Register the business.
2. Connect Stripe.
3. Set opening hours.
4. Photograph the menu.
5. Check what BudeFood has created.
6. Approve it.
7. Add your own photos if wanted.
8. Choose collection / delivery.
9. Go live.
10. Accept orders as they arrive.

The aim is that a normal food outlet should be able to get from registration to a working BudeFood storefront without manually typing its whole menu.
