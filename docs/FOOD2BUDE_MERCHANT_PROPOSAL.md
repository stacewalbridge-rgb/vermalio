# Food2Bude — Local Merchant Proposal

## What Food2Bude is
Food2Bude is a local ordering and delivery platform for Bude-area restaurants, cafés, takeaways, bakeries, pubs and later independent shops.

The customer sees participating local businesses in one app, orders from one or more outlets, and receives one coordinated WhyDrive delivery.

## Why join
- Reach local residents and visitors through one Bude-focused app.
- No expensive dedicated terminal required at launch — use a phone, tablet or computer.
- Smart Menu setup: photograph an existing menu and Food2Bude prepares the items and prices for approval.
- Add or amend items by voice, photo or manual entry.
- Merchants control opening hours, busy/closed status, sold-out items and specials.
- WhyDrive delivery can be called when the food is ready.
- Customers can order from multiple outlets in one basket; Food2Bude coordinates one combined driver collection.
- Merchant and delivery ratings are separated, so an issue with a driver does not unfairly reduce the restaurant's food score.
- Merchants can reply to reviews.
- Optional merchant-uploaded food photos only; Food2Bude does not use misleading stock food images.

## Payments
Food2Bude is designed around Stripe Connect.

The intended customer flow is:
1. Customer creates the order.
2. Each outlet accepts or declines its part.
3. The customer is not charged if an outlet declines.
4. Once every participating outlet accepts, payment is attempted.
5. If payment fails, the merchant is told the order has not been paid and it is not confirmed.
6. If payment succeeds, preparation and delivery proceed.

Target merchant commission for the launch model: **around 10%**, subject to final commercial terms and Stripe costs.

## Delivery
Food2Bude is powered by WhyDrive.

For multi-outlet orders, the platform creates one combined delivery route. One driver can collect the different parts at coordinated ready times and deliver the complete order to the customer, instead of sending separate drivers unnecessarily.

Merchants have:
- Accept with preparation time
- Ready in 5 minutes
- Ready now
- Call driver now
- Delay/update preparation time

## Smart Menu
The merchant presses **Smart Menu**, photographs each menu page, and Food2Bude creates a draft menu.

The merchant checks:
- sections
- item names
- descriptions
- prices
- visible dietary information

Nothing is published until the merchant approves it.

Merchants can also say things such as:
> Add cod and chips at £12.95, mushy peas £1.50 and curry sauce £1.50.

Food2Bude turns this into editable draft items.

## Getting started
1. Register the business.
2. Add contact and opening details.
3. Connect Stripe.
4. Photograph or upload the menu.
5. Check the generated draft.
6. Approve the menu.
7. Select collection, WhyDrive delivery, or both.
8. Go live.

The aim is to make joining Food2Bude possible without a restaurant having to manually type out an entire menu.
