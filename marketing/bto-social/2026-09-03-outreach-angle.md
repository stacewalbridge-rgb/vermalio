# Organic outreach angle

Built To Offend is an AI-powered UK greeting-card maker that turns private in-jokes and harmless personal quirks into personalised roast cards, then prints and posts them automatically.

Pitch angles:
- AI meets British birthday-card humour
- anti-generic personalised cards
- custom roast cards generated from in-jokes
- milestone birthday cards that are actually personal

Avoid fake scarcity, fake reviews or pretending fictional examples are real customers.
