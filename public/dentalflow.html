Vamalio DentalFlow

Turn cancelled chair time into booked treatment.

DentalFlow is a receptionist-first capacity recovery system for private, mixed and NHS dental practices. When an appointment is cancelled, it identifies patients who are suitable for the slot, checks duration, treatment type, urgency, location and availability, then helps staff fill the space quickly without manually ringing down a list.

Built for reception and practice teams

A web app that works on the practice computer.

DentalFlow is designed to run in a browser on a receptionist's desktop or laptop, with the same system also accessible from tablets when needed. It can sit alongside the practice-management system rather than requiring staff to replace it.

Chair-time recovery

Match cancellations to patients whose required treatment and appointment length fit the newly available slot.

Urgency-aware

Prioritise emergency or clinically urgent patients when the released capacity is appropriate for them.

Local availability

Factor in patient location, travel time, notice window and stated short-notice availability before offering the slot.

Examples

Different cancellations need different matches.

0160-minute root canal slot

Find patients needing endodontic treatment who are clinically appropriate and can attend in time.

0230-minute emergency slot

Prioritise urgent pain, swelling or broken-tooth cases that fit the appointment type and clinician.

0320-minute examination

Offer to patients waiting for routine examinations or review appointments.

04Hygiene cancellation

Fill from patients waiting for hygiene appointments without mixing incompatible appointment types.

05Accepted slot

Lock the booking, close competing offers and return the result to the practice workflow.

Commercial model

Suitable for a single practice or a national dental group.

Independent practice

Monthly SaaS licence with a simple receptionist dashboard and configurable rules.

Multi-site dental group

Central administration, practice-level permissions and reporting across multiple clinics.

Enterprise integration

API/integration model for larger providers that want DentalFlow connected to existing patient and appointment systems.

Important: DentalFlow should not make clinical decisions. The practice-management or clinical system remains the source of treatment requirements and eligibility; DentalFlow automates the operational matching and contact workflow.
