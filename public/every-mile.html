Utilise Every Mile

Turn more of every journey into useful work.

Empty running costs money, time and fuel. Vermalio's vision is to help operators identify compatible work, return legs and spare-capacity opportunities so fewer miles are wasted.

Return-leg opportunities

Identify where vehicles are likely to return empty and surface compatible work that could fit the journey.

Capacity awareness

Consider available seats, load space, vehicle type and timing rather than treating every vehicle movement as isolated.

Cross-operation intelligence

Build toward systems that can compare demand, location and availability across multiple parts of an operation.

Smarter scheduling

Sequence work with greater awareness of where vehicles are going next and where demand is emerging.

Lower wasted mileage

Reduce unnecessary dead running where suitable work can be matched without compromising service quality.

Better asset use

Help businesses get more value from vehicles already on the road instead of simply adding more capacity.

The bigger idea

Logistics should think beyond the individual job.

A single booking, parcel or delivery is only one part of the picture. Vermalio's long-term direction is to use connected data and AI assistance to understand the wider movement of jobs, vehicles and capacity — helping operators spot opportunities that are difficult to see manually.

This is a development direction, not a promise that every journey can be matched. The goal is to create better visibility and smarter decisions around unused mileage and capacity.
