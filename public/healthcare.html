Healthcare & dental technology

Operational software that works with existing clinical systems.

Vamalio is developing specialist workflow technology focused on measurable operational problems: recovering unused clinical capacity, recovering cancelled and no-show dental chair time, coordinating access and transport, and reducing avoidable manual administration. The aim is not to replace PAS, EPR or practice-management platforms, but to add an intelligent action layer around them.

Design principles

Small intervention. Clear outcome.

Integration first

Existing clinical and operational systems remain the source of truth. Vamalio is designed to consume only the data needed for the workflow and return the outcome.

Minimal data

No unnecessary duplicate patient database. The architecture is designed around transient operational attributes, identifiers and auditable transactions.

Human escalation

Where the system cannot make a safe or valid match, the workflow stops and routes the case to authorised staff instead of forcing automation.

Healthcare product

RecoveryFlow

Automated short-notice clinical capacity recovery. RecoveryFlow is being designed to identify suitable waiting patients when capacity becomes available unexpectedly, assess practical attendance feasibility, coordinate contact, and return a confirmed result to the existing NHS workflow.

01Capacity appears

A cancellation or newly released slot is surfaced by the source system.

02Eligible candidates

The source system provides clinically appropriate candidate tokens and minimum matching attributes.

03Feasibility

Travel time, timing, contact preference and transport dependencies are checked.

04Contact & accept

Suitable patients are contacted through the configured digital or voice route.

05Recover & measure

The confirmed result is returned and the recovered capacity is recorded.

Dental product

DentalFlow

DentalFlow is designed for private, mixed and NHS dental practices to recover cancelled and no-show chair time. It calculates the remaining usable appointment window, identifies suitable patients and treatment durations, checks practical attendance and launches timed offers so practice teams can recover otherwise-lost capacity without replacing the existing practice-management system.

Cancellation recovery

Match a released appointment to compatible patients and treatments before the slot starts.

No-show rescue

Recalculate the remaining chair window when a patient fails to attend and identify shorter appointments that can still fit.

Reception-first workflow

Give practice teams a controlled operational tool with audit trails, configurable rules and measurable recovered chair time.
