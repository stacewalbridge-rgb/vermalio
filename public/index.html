Healthcare product

Vermalio RecoveryFlow

RecoveryFlow is designed to help healthcare organisations recover short-notice clinical capacity that might otherwise go unused. When a suitable appointment becomes available, it can identify eligible candidates, check practical attendance feasibility, coordinate digital or voice contact, and confirm the result back into the existing workflow.
