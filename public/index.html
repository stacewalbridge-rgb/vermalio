Healthcare product

Vamalio RecoveryFlow

RecoveryFlow is designed to help healthcare organisations recover short-notice clinical capacity that might otherwise go unused. When a suitable appointment becomes available, it can identify eligible candidates, check practical attendance feasibility, coordinate digital or voice contact, and confirm the result back into the existing workflow.

Dental product

Vamalio DentalFlow

DentalFlow helps private, mixed and NHS dental practices recover cancelled and no-show chair time. It calculates the usable remaining appointment window, matches suitable patients and treatment durations, checks practical attendance and coordinates timed offers without replacing the practice-management system.
