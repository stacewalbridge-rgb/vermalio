Delete your account

Request deletion of your WhyDrive Driver account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

Email support@vamalio.com from the email address linked to your WhyDrive Driver account. Use the subject WhyDrive Driver account deletion and include your name and registered phone number so we can verify the request.

What will be deleted

After verification, we will delete or anonymise the WhyDrive Driver account and personal information that is no longer required to provide the service, including driver profile information and account identifiers, subject to the retention requirements below.

What may be retained

Some records may need to be retained for a limited period where required for legal, regulatory, accounting, safety, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or legitimate business purposes. Where possible, retained records are minimised and access is restricted.

How long it takes

We aim to acknowledge deletion requests promptly and complete verified requests within 30 days, unless a longer period is permitted or required by law. We may contact you if additional information is needed to verify the account.

Partial data deletion

If you want to request deletion of particular information without closing your account, contact support@vamalio.com and describe the data you want removed. Some information may be required for the app to continue functioning.
