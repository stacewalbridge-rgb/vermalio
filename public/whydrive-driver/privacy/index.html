Privacy Policy

How Vamalio Technologies Limited handles information used by the WhyDrive Driver app.

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Who we are

WhyDrive Driver is operated by Vamalio Technologies Limited. The app is intended for approved professional drivers using the WhyDrive transport network.

Information we process

  • Driver account and profile information, including name, email address, phone number, vehicle details and account identifiers.
  • Precise location information when location features are enabled, for dispatch, journey coordination and customer tracking related to bookings.
  • Booking and journey information, including pickup and destination details, dates, times, status, passenger-related booking details and fare information.
  • In-app messages connected with bookings.
  • Technical, security and diagnostic information needed to operate and protect the service.

How we use information

  • To authenticate drivers and maintain approved driver accounts.
  • To provide bookings, dispatch, navigation, status updates and customer communication.
  • To provide authorised live location information for active bookings.
  • To provide support, investigate faults, prevent misuse and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Service providers

WhyDrive Driver uses trusted technology providers for services such as authentication, cloud databases, maps, navigation and notifications. These providers process information on our behalf to operate the app.

Data security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect information, including encrypted network connections for data transmitted between the app and its online services.

Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above or where required for legal, regulatory, accounting, safety, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution reasons.

Your rights and account deletion

You can request access, correction or deletion of personal information by contacting support@vamalio.com. To request deletion of a WhyDrive Driver account, visit our account deletion page.
