Reducing dead mileage

Why drive empty when your next booking could be waiting?

Taxi operators often complete journeys away from their normal operating area and return without a passenger.

WhyDrive is designed to help participating operators access suitable bookings across the network. For example, a driver completes a journey from Bude to Exeter. Another customer may need a journey from Exeter towards North Cornwall.

Where licensing rules, availability, location and other requirements permit, the WhyDrive system can identify a suitable opportunity that may reduce otherwise-empty mileage — improving vehicle utilisation and generating additional revenue.

Matching journeys are not guaranteed. Not every return journey will be available, profitable or legally permissible for your licence — for example, a hackney carriage can only be pre-booked outside its licensed zone, and private hire work must be booked through a licensed operator.

Automated fairness module

Fair bookings. Smart allocation. No favourites.

WhyDrive Smart Dispatch is designed around transparent, automated job-allocation principles. Nobody — not an owner, a dispatcher or a driver — hand-picks who gets the next WhyDrive booking.

Designed & in pilot testing

The system considers:

  1. Online & availableWhether the driver is online and available.
  2. Licensed & eligibleWhether the driver and vehicle are appropriately licensed and eligible.
  3. Right vehicleWhether the vehicle has suitable seating and luggage capacity.
  4. Sensible pickupDistance from the customer and realistic pickup suitability.
  5. Right timeDriver availability for the requested date and time.
  6. Right areaRelevant service area and operating restrictions.
  7. Recent historyRecent allocation history and opportunities already received.
  8. Fair spreadSuitable allocation across eligible participating operators.

When several drivers are suitable, the system considers proximity and recent job-allocation history to avoid repeatedly favouring the same operator. Long-distance and airport bookings are balanced separately across eligible participating drivers using the same defined fairness rules, so higher-value work is shared fairly too.

A driver who is genuinely unavailable is skipped automatically — that isn't treated as a refusal, and doesn't push them to the back of the queue.

The process is intended to be automated. Legitimate manual intervention may occasionally be needed for exceptional situations such as safety, cancellations or operational failures — and those interventions are recorded too.

The aim: consistent, transparent, non-discriminatory opportunities for every participating operator.

No pay-to-jump-the-queuePaying more never buys priority over other suitable drivers.
No favouritesNo hidden manual favouritism and no fastest-finger race.
Auditable recordsWhyDrive records why each driver was selected or skipped.
Same rules for allThe same technology and rules apply to every participating driver.

The fairness module is currently being proven in an internal pilot. We will not describe allocation as independently verified until it has been tested and audited.

Tariffs & licensing

Pricing that respects the rules you work under.

WhyDrive must respect the licensing and tariff requirements that apply to each participating operator and each journey. Estimates are determined using the applicable legal and commercial pricing rules.

Hackney carriage

Hackney carriage fares are subject to the maximum fares set by the licensing council. In Cornwall, hackney carriages are licensed in six zones, each with its own maximum table of fares. Day, night, weekend and bank holiday rates and permitted extras apply as set out in the relevant table.

The applicable maximum metered fare for the zone and vehicle must be respected. Charging less than the maximum is allowed.

Private hire

Private hire journeys must be pre-booked through a licensed private hire operator, using a licensed driver and vehicle. Private hire fares are not set by the hackney carriage tariff and should be agreed in advance.

In Cornwall, private hire vehicles aren't restricted to a zone and can work across the county, subject to their licence conditions.

Cornwall Council has been reviewing whether to keep its six hackney carriage zones or merge them into a single county-wide zone with one table of fares. We follow the latest published rules and update pricing when they change. Always check the current position on the Cornwall Council taxi and private hire licensing pages. Operators licensed by other councils must follow their own authority's rules.

We never promise a universally fixed fare where the final lawful charge may differ. Tariff compliance is built into the booking system's pricing rules, not just described here.

Operator questions

Questions operators ask us

Do I have to give WhyDrive my existing customers?

No. Your customers remain yours. WhyDrive adds extra booking opportunities from the network; it doesn't take over your existing work.

Can I still take my own bookings?

Yes. You remain free to accept work independently of WhyDrive. Mark yourself unavailable in the driver app when you're busy with your own work and WhyDrive will skip you automatically, without counting it as a refusal.

Does WhyDrive take a commission?

No. You pay a fixed weekly subscription per vehicle — currently the £39 early adoption rate. It isn't a commission on your fares. You receive your own customer journey payments, subject to the final agreed payment arrangements.

How are WhyDrive jobs allocated?

Automatically, using the fairness rules above: availability, licensing and eligibility, vehicle suitability, pickup position, timing, service area and recent allocation history. Only the selected driver receives the offer, and paying more never buys priority.

Who sets the fares?

Customer prices are calculated by the WhyDrive pricing rules, which must comply with the tariff and licensing requirements that apply to the journey and operator — including any council maximum hackney carriage fares.

Which licences do I need?

You must hold the appropriate, valid licences from your licensing authority for your business, drivers and vehicles — hackney carriage, private hire, or both — plus suitable insurance. We'll ask for documents and expiry dates as part of onboarding.

Can my subscription be paused or cancelled?

Yes, in some circumstances — for example if a required document expires, or if you ask to pause or leave. The reasons and notice periods are summarised in our operator terms and will be set out in full in your subscription agreement.

Is WhyDrive available in my area?

WhyDrive is piloting in Bude and North Cornwall and expanding area by area. Send an enquiry wherever you're based — it helps us plan where to open next.
