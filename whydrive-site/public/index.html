Smart technology

The intelligence behind every journey.

WhyDrive is built on Vamalio's transport technology. The software does the heavy lifting, so passengers get answers faster and local drivers spend more time driving.

  1. AI booking assistant

    Dispatch tools turn messages, voice notes, handwritten notes and photos into structured bookings, ready for dispatch staff to check and confirm.

  2. Fair dispatch In pilot

    Automated allocation weighs availability, vehicle, pickup position and recent workload.

  3. Route-aware pricing

    Fares calculated from real road routes and the applicable pricing rules, shown before you book.

  4. Live journey updates

    Booking status, driver details and arrival alerts sent straight to your phone.

The problem we solve

Stop ringing round. Start booking.

Finding a local taxi often means calling one company after another, hoping somebody has a car free. WhyDrive puts participating independent operators behind one simple request.

Customers can request journeys, view fare estimates, see their routes, receive booking updates and communicate with their assigned driver — all through WhyDrive.

WhyDrive is a shared technology platform, not a replacement for independent local taxi businesses. Your journey is provided by a participating licensed operator.

Safety & trust

Know who's coming. Keep your details private.

Every booking shows your assigned driver and vehicle so you can check them before you get in.

  • Check the vehicle and registration match your booking. If they don't, don't get in — report it in the app.
  • Message your driver through WhyDrive instead of sharing personal numbers.
  • Cancel, report lost property or request a receipt from My Bookings.
  • Each participating operator is responsible for its own licensed vehicles and drivers.

In an emergency, always call 999.
